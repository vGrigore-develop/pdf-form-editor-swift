# Third-party notices

This repository does not vendor third-party source code. The components below are either downloaded at build time, installed into local, gitignored environments, or (for one font) included as a test fixture.

## Linked into the app

**MuPDF 1.28.2** (Artifex Software, Inc.), GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later. https://mupdf.com
Downloaded and built by `scripts/build-mupdf.sh` (pinned version and SHA-256) into `vendor/`, which is gitignored, and linked statically into the app. Because the app links MuPDF, anyone distributing a build of the app, or making it available over a network, must do so under terms compatible with the AGPL-3.0, including offering the complete corresponding source. Commercial MuPDF licences are available from Artifex.

The `libmupdf-third.a` library built from the MuPDF source release bundles MuPDF's own third-party dependencies, each under its own licence (see `thirdparty/` in the MuPDF source release): FreeType (FreeType License), HarfBuzz (MIT), jbig2dec (AGPL-3.0), lcms2 (MIT), libjpeg (IJG), OpenJPEG (BSD-2-Clause), zlib (zlib), Gumbo (Apache-2.0), extract (AGPL-3.0), Brotli (MIT), MuJS (ISC; the JavaScript engine is compiled out with `FZ_ENABLE_JS=0`), and the fonts in `resources/fonts/` (SIL OFL 1.1, URW and others as stated there).
